How I will measure the campaign

I will use campaign and creative UTMs, validate website events, and reconcile genuine hiring enquiries and accepted interviews with a private tracker. Public reports will show date range, spend, outcome definitions, attribution settings and last refresh time. Personal contact details will never appear here.

Automated reporting requires an authorised Meta reporting connection and a protected server-side refresh. Until that is connected, I will publish dated, verified snapshots rather than label manual numbers “live”.
